Welcome to Gram Panchayat

This is the official website of the Gram Panchayat.

Our Gram Panchayat is committed to the welfare and development of our village and its residents.

➡Vision::

To build a self-reliant, clean, and progressive village that thrives on community participation and development.

➡Mission::

To ensure the implementation of rural development schemes, maintain basic amenities, and promote education, health, and employment opportunities for all.

➡Key Development Highlights::

  • Clean drinking water supply
  • Clean drinking water supply Clean drinking water supply is ensured for every household. Pipelines and water tanks are regularly maintained. Safe drinking water is our utmost priority. We have implemented water harvesting techniques to conserve water. Special focus on hygiene and health benefits of pure water. Continuous monitoring to maintain quality standards.
  • Proper sanitation and waste management::
  • Proper sanitation and waste management facilities are developed. Daily waste collection and disposal mechanisms are followed. Focus on making the village open defecation free. Awareness drives are conducted on hygiene and cleanliness. Waste segregation is promoted at the source level.
  • Government scheme awareness drives::
  • Government scheme awareness drives are organized regularly. People are informed about schemes like PM Awas Yojana, Ujjwala Yojana, etc. Village notice boards and meetings are used for updates. Volunteers assist villagers in availing benefits. Special focus on women's and farmers' welfare schemes. We ensure maximum benefit reach to every household.
  • Health camps and women empowerment programs::
  • Health camps are organized regularly for villagers. Free check-ups and medicines are provided. Focus on women empowerment through various programs. Skill development sessions are arranged for women. Special awareness about maternal and child health. Ensuring better health and a brighter future for all.
  • Educational support for children::
  • Educational support is provided to children from poor backgrounds. Scholarships and study materials are distributed. Focus on increasing literacy and reducing dropouts. Special coaching for competitive exams is arranged. Facilities like libraries and e-learning centers developed. Empowering the young minds to build a brighter future."

----------About Us----------

Our Gram Panchayat is the backbone of local governance, dedicated to serving the needs of the village people.

Established in 1985, our Panchayat has continuously worked towards the welfare and development of the village, focusing on infrastructure, education, healthcare, and social initiatives.

The Panchayat consists of elected members who represent the voice of the people and work towards solving local issues with transparency and efficiency.

Our goal is to build a progressive, inclusive, and sustainable village where every citizen can live with dignity and access basic amenities.

We believe in active participation of the community and strive to empower our citizens through various development programs, awareness campaigns, and government schemes.

----------Our Services----------

Issuance of birth certificates for newborns with accurate records.

Issuance of official death certificates for legal purposes.

Manage property tax payments to support village development projects.

Ensure regular and clean water supply to every household in the village.

Maintain cleanliness, sanitation, and proper waste disposal in the community.

Help villagers apply for various state and central government schemes.

Organize health camps, promote education, and support school development.

----------Schemes & Programs----------

We assist villagers in availing the benefits of various state and central government schemes aimed at improving their living standards.

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) - Housing for all eligible villagers.

Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) - Provides 100 days of guaranteed wage employment.

Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) - Financial inclusion by opening bank accounts.

Swachh Bharat Abhiyan - Promoting sanitation and cleanliness in the village.

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) - Financial assistance to farmers.

Pension Schemes - Assistance for senior citizens, widows, and disabled persons.

Girl Child Schemes - Support schemes for girl child education and welfare.
